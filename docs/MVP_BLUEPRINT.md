# Give Back to School — MVP Blueprint v1.0

## Core promise
Schools tell us what they need. You choose what you want to give.

## Mission
To connect verified Philippine public-school needs with people who want to give back, through a simple, protected and transparent process where givers provide needed goods directly and every fulfilment can be followed through completion.

## Vision
A Philippines where teachers can make legitimate school needs visible without personally soliciting strangers, and where anyone—from a former student nearby to an OFW across the world—can confidently give something useful back to a school.

## Locked MVP
Teacher verification → approved physical-goods need → giver commitment → giver buys compliant item from supplier of choice → in-platform communication → purchase/delivery proof → teacher receipt confirmation → completed project.

No donation money passes through GBTS. No teacher cash/GCash/bank requests. Teacher identity is appropriately public after verification while private details stay hidden. Givers choose public, limited, corporate, or anonymous recognition.

## Founder principle
The founder handles verification, approval, exceptions and disputes. Routine tracking should be automated by the platform.
